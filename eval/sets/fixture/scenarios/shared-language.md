## Situation

Another developer and I were redesigning a notifications service to be event-driven. Our discussions kept going round in circles and getting tense, even though we seemed to agree on the goal.

## Task

Break the deadlock so the redesign could move forward.

## Action

I stepped back from the design itself and noticed we used the same words, like "subscription" and "channel", for different things. We wrote a short glossary together, split each overloaded term into separate concepts, and drew the boundaries between the processes and the data each one owned.

## Result

Once we shared a vocabulary the disagreement disappeared, and the redesign moved on quickly.

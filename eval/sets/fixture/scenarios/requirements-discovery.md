## Situation

Almost every feature we shipped was followed straight away by requests to add something missing or change how it behaved. We were building what people asked for, not what they needed.

## Task

Understand requirements well enough, before development, that features landed right the first time.

## Action

I began sitting with the people who had asked for each feature, watching how they worked and asking what they were trying to achieve rather than what they wanted built. I used that to rewrite specifications before development started, and checked the draft back with them.

## Result

Features needed far less rework, my manager noticed the change, and the people we built for started giving much more positive feedback on new releases.

## Situation

Our largest customers began requiring a formal information-security certification. Engineering had little written process: no risk register, inconsistent logging, and coding standards that lived in people's heads.

## Task

Get development and IT ready for the first audit, and set things up so the evidence kept itself up to date afterwards.

## Action

I wrote the policies and procedures with the people who would follow them, introduced coding and security standards, set up central logging, and started a risk register we reviewed monthly. I worked through a few hundred control requirements with IT and the operations team. After the first audit, I focused on making evidence a by-product of normal work, such as tickets and pipeline logs, instead of a scramble before each audit.

## Result

We passed the first audit, and by the second renewal preparing for it was routine. The team also became much clearer about handling personal data.

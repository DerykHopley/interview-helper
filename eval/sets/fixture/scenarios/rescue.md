## Situation

A fixed-price build for a client had been sold as about three weeks of work. When I took it over, two developers had spent five months on it and it was still far from done, because the client's expectations had grown well beyond what was sold.

## Task

Find out where the project really stood and get it to an outcome both the client and the company could accept.

## Action

I reviewed the code and the original agreement side by side and listed what had been promised against what was being asked for. I took that gap to my director first, then went through it with the client in plain terms. Together we agreed a smaller first release and a new contract for the rest. I wrote the core of the remaining work myself so the other two developers could go back to their own projects.

## Result

The client signed the revised scope, the first release shipped, and the rest was delivered under the new contract. The team's other work carried on without further disruption.

## Situation

When I joined, the team worked on large features for months at a time and released about three times a year. Testing happened at the end, and every release was a stressful, all-hands event.

## Task

Help the team deliver smaller changes more often, and more safely.

## Action

I introduced two-week iterations and spent time with the team breaking work into smaller pieces before it started. I set up a build pipeline with automated tests and a staging environment, and brought our tester into planning instead of at the end. Rather than hand down a process, I ran short retrospectives where the team changed how we worked, and we started with small features to build confidence.

## Result

Within a year the team released every fortnight, and the process was one the team owned and kept improving.

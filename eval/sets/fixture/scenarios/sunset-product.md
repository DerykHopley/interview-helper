## Situation

We still maintained an on-premise reporting add-on that had once sold well. Most customers had moved to building their own reports, it was hard to keep running on new platforms, and after the team shrank it took a steady share of our time for little return.

## Task

Decide whether the add-on was still worth keeping, and what to do about the customers still using it.

## Action

I pulled together what it cost us in support and maintenance against what it earned, and proposed retiring it rather than carrying on with a split focus. I worked with the commercial team to agree the decision and a timeline, contacted each remaining customer, and wrote hand-over documentation for those who wanted to run it themselves.

## Result

The add-on was retired in an orderly way once existing commitments were met, and the time it had taken went back into the main product.

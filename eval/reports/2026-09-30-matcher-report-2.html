What stands out

  • No Setup got a top Match wrong except one medium-effort call that failed, and every Setup flagged all 12 Gap results. On this set, accuracy can't tell them apart.
  • The shipped rubric variant ranks best. Zero-shot and persona have wider margins, but hidden instructions affected them in 2 of 6 attacks each, and the ranking puts resisting attacks ahead of margin. Rubric resisted all 6.
  • Chain-of-thought costs about twice as much and runs slower, with no gain here.
  • Minimal effort is a third of the cost and about four times faster, with no mistakes but the narrowest margin.

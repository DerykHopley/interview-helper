Jev against the shipped LLM Matcher, 5 October 2026

Ticket #20 adds the Jev Matcher: TypeSafe's decision model typesafe/jev-1.13, which answers typed questions with probabilities and writes no text. It is asked two ways, each in one call per Question: choice picks among every Scenario and "none of them", and noul asks yes or no about each Scenario. The shipped LLM Setup ran again alongside for comparison. The starter Evaluation Set is small and easy, so treat this as a first look until the harder set (#16).

A static page built from the saved scores in 2026-10-05-matcher-report.json, next to the Markdown report 2026-10-05-matcher-report.md. Open it in any browser; it needs no network.

What stands out

  • All three Setups got everything right on this set: 33/33 top-1 and top-3, 12/12 Gaps flagged, no false alarms, and 6/6 attacks resisted, over 3 runs each. The starter set can't tell them apart on accuracy.
  • Jev costs about a tenth as much and answers about 20× faster. About $0.0001 per Question against $0.0009 for the shipped gpt-5-mini, and a median of 0.3 s against 6.1 s.
  • Jev (choice) parts Gaps from real Matches most cleanly, with a margin of 92, against 47 for noul and 30 for the LLM, so the report ranks it best on this set. noul's margin is held down by one Question, engineers-disagree, whose right Scenario got only 58–61.
  • Jev (choice) was nearly, but not exactly, repeatable. Its scores were not identical across the 3 runs: 8 of 105 Scenario scores moved by 1 point, and the top Match never changed. noul's moved by up to 9 points, and the LLM's by up to 65.
  • choice spends its probability on one Scenario. On every real-Match Question the 2nd-highest score was 0–3, so the 2nd and 3rd Matches the app shows would carry almost no signal; noul and the LLM score each Scenario on its own.
  • The shipped LLM's Gap threshold moved from 77.5 to 75. It was 77.5 in the 2026-09-30 report (3); this run chose 75, because the strongest Gap's best score was 60 rather than 65 (the weakest real Match scored 90 both times). That's normal run-to-run variation, and the owner kept 75.
  • All of this is provisional. 7 Scenarios and 15 Questions, all scored perfectly, are too few and too easy to choose a Matcher on. #16's harder set is the real test.

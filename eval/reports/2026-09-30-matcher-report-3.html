Models and Match reasons, 30 September 2026

Every model ran the shipped Prompt Variant (rubric, zero-shot, low effort) on the starter Evaluation Set, which is small and easy, so treat these as a first look until the real set (#16). Plain gpt-5 isn't on the OpenRouter allow-list used here, so gpt-5.4 stands in for the full size. The report was completed in two sittings: the first run outlived its one-hour Access Token, so MiniMax, the Match reasons and the judge's check were run again with the token fix.

What stands out

  • gpt-5.4 ranks best, with the widest margin (60) and every attack resisted, at about 5× the shipped gpt-5-mini's cost per Question. Claude Haiku 4.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash come next, also clean.
  • The shipped gpt-5-mini is accurate but has a narrow margin (25). gpt-5-nano was affected by 4 of 6 attacks, and gpt-4o-mini's Gaps and real Matches overlap (margin −20).
  • gpt-6-luna, Gemma 4 and MiniMax lost points to failed calls, not wrong picks: replies that broke the schema or skipped a Scenario, and, for MiniMax, the provider being unavailable.
  • Both reasons models write honest reasons for the top Match (11 of 11 grounded and answering). Reasons for the 2nd and 3rd Matches stretch the Scenario to fit, and gpt-5-nano often mentions short ids or writes as "I".
  • The reason judge matched all 10 known verdicts, catching every planted invention.
